A villa with a garden in Mentana, to visit Rome and the surrounding countryside without sleeping in the middle of the traffic. The area is residential and quiet, and the bus stop is 300 metres away.

## Ways to stay

- **B&B with breakfast**, which you can have outdoors in the garden.
- **A room by the night**, for one or more nights.
- **A whole floor** with a kitchen, for families or groups of friends: 3 bedrooms and 6 beds.
- **The whole villa**, up to 18 beds, on request.

## The garden

Tables, a relaxing area, a barbecue and parking: a place to come back to in the evening after a day out.
