A room in a quiet house, with a kitchen to cook your meals and a garden to study in when the weather is nice. The villa is in Mentana, in the Mezzaluna area, near Rome, with the bus stop 300 metres away.

## What you get

- **Rooms in a villa on three floors**, each with a living room, a kitchen, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and a cellar.
- **Kitchen and living room** for whoever lives on the floor.
- **The garden** with tables and a relaxing area, to study outdoors or to spend time with friends.
- **Stays of several months**, even for the whole academic year.

## How it works

Tell us from when you need the room, for how long and whether there are several of you looking together. We will reply with availability and conditions. If there are many of you, you can also book a whole floor with 6 beds.
