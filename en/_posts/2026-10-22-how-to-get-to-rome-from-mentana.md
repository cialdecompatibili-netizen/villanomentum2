Staying in Mentana and visiting Rome is easy to do without stress: the centre is about 20 km away. Here are the most convenient routes, with approximate times.

## In short

- **Direct Cotral bus**: from Mentana to Roma Tiburtina in about 50 minutes, for a few euros.
- **FL1 train**: from Monterotondo-Mentana station to Roma Tiburtina in about 35 minutes, with frequent services.
- **Car**: about half an hour without traffic, much more at rush hour, and in Rome it is best to leave it outside the centre.

Times are approximate and come from public journey planners: before leaving, always check the updated timetables on Cotral and Trenitalia.

## By bus: the simplest option

Cotral lines run from Mentana to **Roma Tiburtina** and **Roma Ponte Mammolo**. These are the two stations that matter, because from both you take **metro line B** into the centre: Colosseo, Termini and Circo Massimo are all on the same line.

The advantage of the bus is that you do not have to change to reach Rome, and the ticket is cheap. The downside is that, as on any road into Rome, traffic at rush hour can make the trip longer.

## By train: the FL1

The station is called **Monterotondo-Mentana** and is on the **FL1** line, which goes to Roma Tiburtina and continues towards the centre and Fiumicino airport. The train is the best choice if you want more predictable times, because it does not get stuck in traffic.

The station is in Monterotondo, so from the villa you first need a short bus ride to the station. When you book, write to us: we will tell you which lines to take from the stop 300 metres from the villa.

## What about the airport?

With the FL1 you can reach Fiumicino without going through the centre of Rome, but check the connections: they depend on the time of day and on any works on the line.

## Practical tips

1. **Check the last evening service** before going out: if you come back late, it is better to know in advance.
2. **Leave early**: breakfast in the garden and off you go, to be in Rome before the queues.
3. **Tiburtina is also handy for changing**: from here you can take metro B and trains to other cities.
4. **Car**: if you have one, use the park-and-ride car parks outside the centre in Rome.
