You don't need the Alps to see snow: from Rome you can reach several resorts in the central Apennines in a day, some in Lazio and some just across the border in Abruzzo. Drive times from the capital range from about an hour and a half to two hours, depending on the resort and weekend traffic.

## In Lazio

### Monte Livata (province of Rome)
The closest resort to Rome, about an hour and a half away, and a favourite for beginners and families. It has 5 slopes served by 4 lifts, a Family Park at Campo dell'Osso and groomed cross-country trails. In winter you can also snowshoe, on routes up to about 1,550 metres, by day or at sunset.

### Terminillo (province of Rieti)
The historic ski resort known as Rome's mountain, under two hours by car. Lifts run between Terminilluccio and Terminillo, up to about 2,100 metres. It is also a good choice for snowshoeing, with routes of different difficulty.

### Campo Staffi (Filettino, province of Frosinone)
A quieter and often cheaper resort, with alpine slopes and three cross-country loops. The longest, about 20 km, links Campo Staffi to Monte Livata.

### Campo Catino (Guarcino, province of Frosinone)
Another Lazio resort, among the quickest to reach from Rome and suited to a family day out.

## Just across the border, in Abruzzo

### Campo Felice
A karst plateau in the Sirente-Velino regional park, easy to reach on the Rome-L'Aquila motorway. Part of the "Tre Nevi" area with Ovindoli and Campo Imperatore, with about 30 km of alpine slopes. Besides the day pass there are hourly passes, handy for a quick trip.

### Ovindoli
The Monte Magnola area runs from about 1,400 to 2,200 metres, with about 30 km of slopes and modern lifts. It has snowparks and, on some days of the week, night skiing under lights. The Altopiano delle Rocche offers some of the longest cross-country trails in central Italy.

### Campo Imperatore (Gran Sasso)
The first ski resort developed in the Apennines. You go up from Fonte Cerreto by cable car, then by chairlift, for very scenic slopes. The plateau is also a cross-country classic.

## Practical tips

- **Check snow and lifts before leaving.** Snow varies a lot from year to year: always check the resort's official site and webcams.
- **Leave early on weekends.** Access roads clog up, especially for Monte Livata and Terminillo.
- **Carry snow chains or winter tyres.** They are often compulsory on mountain roads.
- **Not only skiing.** Snowshoeing, cross-country skiing and sledging are cheap alternatives for non-skiers.
