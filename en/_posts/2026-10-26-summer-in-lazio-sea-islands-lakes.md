The Lazio coast changes character from north to south. The beaches closest to Rome are handy for a day trip, but the most scenic sea is in the province of Latina and on the Pontine Islands.

## Close to Rome

**Anzio** (about 60 km south of Rome) and **Santa Marinella** are the easiest seaside escapes from the city, good for families too. For history rather than swimming, the **Ostia Antica Archaeological Park** is one of the best-preserved sites in Italy.

## Riviera di Ulisse and Circeo

- **Sabaudia and San Felice Circeo:** inside the Circeo National Park, with sandy beaches edged by dunes and coastal lakes (Paola, Monaci, Caprolace).
- **Terracina:** about 15 km of fine sand between the Circeo and Gaeta headlands.
- **Sperlonga:** a white village on the sea with pale sandy beaches and equipped beach clubs. In summer, parking near the beaches is mostly paid and demand is high around midday.
- **Gaeta:** seven beaches, including Serapo and Sant'Agostino bay. Try the tiella. The 300 Steps beach (Arenauta), on the road between Gaeta and Sperlonga, is spectacular.

## Pontine Islands

Ponza and Ventotene are reached by ferry and hydrofoil from Formia, Anzio, Terracina and, in summer, San Felice Circeo. Many of Ponza's coves are reachable only by sea: Cala Feola and Frontone are among the best known, great for snorkelling. Chiaia di Luna can now only be admired from the sea.

## To cool off: the lakes

**Lake Bolsena** is the largest volcanic lake in Europe, while **Lake Bracciano** is an easy trip from Rome for a swim and a day outdoors.

## Practical tips

- **Leave early** on weekends: roads and car parks fill up.
- **For the islands,** check ferry and hydrofoil timetables and bookings.
- **Check** beach services before you go.
