If you work remotely or study in Rome, you do not necessarily need a room in the centre. What often matters more is having a quiet place to concentrate, a real kitchen and an easy way to get into the city when needed.

## Why outside Rome

- **Quiet**: the villa is in Mentana, in the Mezzaluna area, in a residential neighbourhood. No street noise under your window.
- **Real spaces**: every floor has a living room, a kitchen, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and a cellar. You cook, you eat at a table, you study in the living room.
- **The garden**: tables and a relaxing area for breaks or for working outdoors.
- **Rome when you need it**: bus and train take you to Roma Tiburtina, where you take metro B. Times and routes are in the guide on [how to get to Rome from Mentana]({% include url-ref.html ref="come-arrivare" %}).

## For remote workers

A typical day can be simple: breakfast, hours of work in the living room or in the garden, lunch in the kitchen, a walk around the neighbourhood. A couple of days a week you can go to Rome for meetings, without paying for a room in the centre all month.

Tell us what you need to work (how many days, how many people, what kind of space): we will reply with the possible solutions, from a room to a whole floor. You will find the details on the page for [remote workers]({% include url-ref.html ref="smart-worker" %}).

## For students

If you study in Rome, the advantage is having a home where you can rest and study in peace. Several university sites, such as La Sapienza, are served by **metro B**, reachable from Tiburtina. Always check the exact route to your faculty before choosing where to stay.

If there are several of you, you can also book a whole floor with 6 beds, with a shared kitchen and living room. See the page for [students]({% include url-ref.html ref="studentesse" %}).

## How it works

Stays are for **weeks or months**. Tell us from when you need the room, for how long and how many of you there are: we will reply with availability and conditions.
