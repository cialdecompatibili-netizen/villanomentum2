Spring is Lazio at its best: mild temperatures, blossoms and less heat than summer. March is quieter, while April and May are the busiest on weekends.

## Gardens and blooms

### Ninfa Garden (Cisterna di Latina)
Among medieval ruins and the river running through them, it is considered one of the most beautiful gardens in Italy. It opens only at certain times of year, usually on weekends and public holidays until November, and **booking is required**. Allow about an hour and a half. It pairs well with the nearby village of Sermoneta.

### Sermoneta
A medieval village in the province of Latina, about 70 km from Rome, with the well-preserved Caetani Castle. The centre is compact and walkable; on spring weekends it is best to book ahead.

### The villas of Tivoli
Villa d'Este (Renaissance, terraced gardens and fountains), Villa Gregoriana and Villa Adriana are UNESCO sites. Booking is recommended for Villa d'Este, and arriving early on weekends helps. Mid-April is the time for blooms.

## Lakes and villages

- **Castelli Romani:** lakes Albano and Nemi are ideal for easy spring walks, minutes from Rome.
- **Lake Bolsena:** the largest volcanic lake in Europe, in Tuscia, with villages such as Capodimonte and Montefiascone and lakeside fish restaurants.
- **Civita di Bagnoregio:** the "suspended" village, free to visit.

## Practical tips

- **Book limited-entry places** (Ninfa, Villa d'Este) well in advance.
- **Check opening dates and hours** on official sites: they change every year.
- **A car is best** for villages and parks; Tivoli and the Castelli Romani are reachable by public transport.
