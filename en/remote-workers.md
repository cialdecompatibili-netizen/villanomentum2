Working from home works better when the home is the right one. The villa is in Mentana, in the Mezzaluna area, in a quiet residential neighbourhood, with the bus stop 300 metres away.

## What you get

- **A floor to live in**: every floor has a living room, a kitchen, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and a cellar.
- **A real kitchen** at your disposal, so you can have lunch at home without going out.
- **The garden** for breaks: tables, a relaxing area and a barbecue in the evening.
- **Stays of weeks or months**, without the constraints of a long contract.

## How it works

Write to us with your start date, how long you plan to stay and what you need. We will reply with availability and conditions. You can choose a single room or a whole floor if there are several of you.
